## Users

## Product Purpose

เว็บไซต์ประชาสัมพันธ์ของโรงพยาบาล (Hospital PR and announcement website) — to inform, communicate, and provide access to hospital services and news clearly and effectively.

## Positioning

A formal, reliable, and trustworthy digital front door for the hospital, ensuring that the general public can access health information and announcements with confidence.

## Operating Context

General public browsing from home or mobile devices, often seeking important health information, news, or hospital services, expecting clear navigation and immediate trust.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Must be responsive (mobile-friendly), highly accessible, and project formal reliability (ความน่าเชื่อถือแบบทางการ). 

## Brand Commitments

No strict restrictions on brand assets, but the overarching tone must be formal and highly trustworthy.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing React components (HomePage, AnnouncementDetailPage, PdfViewer) providing the structure for news, announcements, and document viewing.

## Product Principles

1. **Clarity over Cleverness:** Information must be immediately legible and straightforward.
2. **Authoritative Trust:** The visual language must project institutional stability, cleanliness, and safety.
3. **Universal Accessibility:** As a public health resource, it must be easily usable across devices and demographics.
